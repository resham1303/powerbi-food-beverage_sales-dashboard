Food & Beverage Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

This interactive Food & Beverage Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance across revenue, profit, and product segments. It is designed to help stakeholders quickly analyze key metrics, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

 Key Highlights
Overall Performance
Profit Margin: 64.99%
Total Revenue: $10.21M
Total Profit: $6.64M
Revenue Breakdown
By Product Category: Majority revenue comes from Food (~90%), with Drinks contributing ~9%
By Channel: Highest sales from Retail, followed by Distributor and Online
Product Insights
Top-performing product group: Wheat Flour
Other contributors include Oil, Yeasts, Flour, and Liquor
Manager Performance
Detailed view of revenue, profit, and margin by manager
Enables comparison of individual contributions and efficiency
Time Trends
Monthly revenue trend shows steady growth, peaking towards the later months of the year
Dynamic Filtering
Year slicer (2019–2021) allows users to analyze performance across different time periods
 Business Value

This dashboard helps:

Identify high-performing products and channels
Track profitability across teams and managers
Understand seasonal trends in revenue
Enable faster, data-driven decision making
 Insights You Can Draw
Retail is the dominant revenue channel
Food products are the primary revenue driver
Revenue shows seasonal growth patterns
Profit margins remain consistently strong (~65%)
